## Colours (owner request 2026-10-22)

The source files are single-colour. They are shown in each maker's brand colour: Honda #E40521, Toyota #EB0A1E, Mitsubishi #E60012,
Nissan #C3002F, GMC #CE1126, Ford #003478 (white script cut out of the blue oval), Chevrolet #CD9834 (gold bowtie), Hyundai #002C5F,
Mazda #101010 and Mercedes-Benz #242424 (both use dark/silver emblems). Dodge was already in colour (black wordmark, red stripes).
BMW is layered from the same Simple Icons shape: black ring, white letters, blue (#0066B1) and white quarters.
Only the fill colours were changed; the shapes are the original files.
